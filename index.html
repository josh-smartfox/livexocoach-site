Defenders read and react: man coverage follows your receivers, zones pass them off, blitzers come after the QB. You see what happens before game day, not after.

Design plays in seconds

Formations for 5v5 NFL Flag, 6v6, 7v7 and 4v4. Route tree, freehand routes, motion, jet sweeps, reverses and play-action, with the QB's reads set exactly.

Run them against a live defense
