Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LiveXO Coach is a play designer and football teaching app for coaches. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your playbooks stay on your device, and we don't collect personal information.

What stays on your device

Your playbooks, plays, opponent scouting reports, lesson progress and settings are saved only on your phone or tablet. We don't run servers that store them, and we can't see them.

What we don't collect

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to:

  • Sharing. When you share a playbook file, a video or a GIF, it goes to the app you pick (Messages, email, etc.). What happens next is up to that app and the people you send it to.
  • Printing. Printing uses your device's print service.
  • TV. Showing a lesson or drive on a TV uses your device's screen mirroring or display connection.
  • Read aloud. Reading text aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine, set in your device's settings.

Purchases

LiveXO Coach Pro is sold through Google Play and the Apple App Store, which handle payment. We never see your card details. To keep track of your Pro access across reinstalls, our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app ID and the details of your purchase (which plan, when, and whether it's active). RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

LiveXO Coach is meant for coaches and parents. Players may use it with a coach or parent, for example during a team lesson on a TV. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on our website, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email support@livexocoach.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app.
